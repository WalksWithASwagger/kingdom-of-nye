# assets/ — audio credits

## nye-nightwaves.mp3

Original synthesized ambience for The Kingdom of Nye ("Nightwaves") — a 3 a.m.
shortwave dial: colored-noise static, detuned heterodyne carriers, sparse
numbers-station blips, and a low rumble.

Generated **from scratch, no samples**, by [`make_nightwaves.py`](./make_nightwaves.py)
(deterministic, seed 1140). Released to the public domain under **CC0 1.0** — no rights
reserved, no attribution required.

## No Art Bell audio is bundled

No Art Bell / Coast to Coast AM audio is included or hosted anywhere in this repo — it
is copyrighted (Premiere Networks / iHeartMedia). To hear real episodes, use the
player's **drag-and-drop** with files you own, or the **"find episodes"** links to
archive.org and the official Art Bell Vault on YouTube.
